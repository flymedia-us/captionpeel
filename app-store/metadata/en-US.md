## Promotional text

## Description

CaptionPeel turns captions already visible in your video into an editable SRT subtitle file.

Open a video, draw around the caption area, and let CaptionPeel detect the visible text and time each cue. Review and edit the results, then export a standard UTF-8 SRT file for your editing, archival, or publishing workflow.

Everything happens locally on your Mac. CaptionPeel does not upload your videos, captions, or usage data. It uses Apple’s on-device frameworks to read the caption region you select.

Features:

- Extract burned-in captions from video into SRT
- Select and resize the caption area for each video
- Keep cue boundaries aligned to the video’s frames
- Review, edit, merge, delete, and re-read cues
- Export standard multiline UTF-8 SRT files
- Work entirely offline, with no account or cloud service

CaptionPeel extracts text that is already visible in video. It does not transcribe audio, translate subtitles, burn captions into video, or replace a full subtitle-authoring timeline.
