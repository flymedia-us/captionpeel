# App Review Information

Paste the following into App Review Notes:

> CaptionPeel does not require an account, login, subscription, special hardware, or a network connection. To test the core flow, open any local video file, draw a rectangle around burned-in captions, select Extract Captions, review the generated cues, and export an SRT file. Video frames and recognized text are processed locally on the Mac; no content is uploaded.
