# CaptionPeel Privacy Policy

**Effective September 9, 2026**

CaptionPeel is developed by Fly Media LLC. The app is designed to perform its work entirely on your Mac.

## Data collection

CaptionPeel does not collect personal information, analytics, diagnostics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

## Video and subtitle processing

CaptionPeel accesses only video files you explicitly open or drop into the app. Video frames and recognized subtitle text are processed locally with Apple frameworks. They are not uploaded to Fly Media or any third party.

CaptionPeel writes an SRT file only when you choose a destination in the macOS save panel.

## Network access

CaptionPeel does not require or initiate network connections during normal operation.

## Retention

CaptionPeel does not maintain a cloud service or server-side database and therefore retains no user data. Closing the app discards the current in-memory extraction session unless you exported it as an SRT file.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document and its effective date.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to Fly Media through the support channel listed with the distributed app.
